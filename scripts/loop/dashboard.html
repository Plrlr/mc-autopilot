A Minecraft bot that teaches itself

Every generation plays fresh random worlds on GitHub's machines. Challengers race the champion on the same worlds, and only a clear win takes the crown. Every decision it makes also trains its own model. Nobody tunes it by hand.

Mean score of the champion on that generation's fresh worlds (x axis: generation; from generation 5 on, stage starts are left out: they're on the road to the dragon). Gray dots are challengers. Points come from milestones and steps toward the Nether portal (more for reaching them early), minus deaths. Stars mark a new champion.

The road to the dragon

Runs save the world when they first reach a stage. Every generation also starts some runs from those saved worlds at the frontier (outlined), so later stages get practice before the early game is perfect. The bar shows how often a start at that stage got past it. "Staged" means a test-world setup, used until real saves exist.

How far the runs get

Share of each generation's runs from a fresh world that reached a step (all genomes, 20 game minutes each). From generation 5 on; earlier points also count the stage starts.

What the champion changed

Each gene the champion has moved away from the hand-tuned default, as a share of the gene's allowed range.

Its own model

A model trained only on this bot's runs: which action pays off in which situation. It can reorder the rules' options only as far as the champion's learned.weight allows.

Skills

How each action works out over every game the loop has played (brain v2's skill stats): tries, share that succeeded, deaths during it, and the usual time of a success. The strategist picks routes by these; the worst ones come first, since that's where a fix pays most.

Family tree

Every genome tried, placed by the generation it was born in. Lines join a child to its parent. Blue marks the champions' line of descent, orange marks challengers still racing, and gray marks dropped ones.
